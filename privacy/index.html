Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Moto Tools is a brand of Grayco Tools LLC in Utah. This page says, in plain words, what we keep about your rider, who can see it, and how to get it deleted. Questions: hello@mototoolsmx.com.

What we keep

  • Rides from your GPS box. Where the bike went, how fast, and when. A RaceBox also records motion (used to spot jumps and falls).
  • The rider's page. What you type in: first name, number, plate colors, class, notes, and race results you paste in.
  • Sign-in. Each family has its own password. Your phone or computer keeps you signed in.

Where it's kept

Rides and rider pages are kept on our ride server at app.mototoolsmx.com, which is hosted by Replit. Each family's rides are kept in their own space. Kam keeps backup copies so a ride isn't lost.

What stays on your phone

To make a video or photo with the stats on it, the iPhone app asks to see your Photos. The video or photo is made on your phone and stays there. We don't upload your photos or videos. The app uses Bluetooth only to talk to your GPS box.

Who can see it

  • Your family: anyone with your family's password sees your rider, and only your rider.
  • Kam, who runs Moto Tools, to keep it working and fix problems.
  • A trainer or coach only when you tell us to share with them.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with advertisers. Maps are drawn with OpenStreetMap, so their map server sees which area of the map is being shown. This site's fonts come from Google Fonts, so Google sees that the page was opened.

Training sessions

If your rider uses one of our boxes at a trainer's session, we ask you first. With your yes, the ride is shown on the trainer's screen to that session's group, and it's kept in a private space for that program. Afterward we move it to your own rider's page if you want it, or delete it. Nothing from a session is posted without a separate yes.

Posts and promotion

We only use a rider's data, name or photos on Instagram or this site after that rider's parent says yes in writing (a text counts). Riders 18 and over say yes for themselves. You can take that yes back at any time and we'll stop using it.

Kids

Moto Tools is for parents, trainers and older riders. A parent or guardian sets up each rider's page and decides who sees it. Children don't create their own accounts.

Home and the drive in

The box records wherever it's switched on, including your yard or the drive to the track. Your rider's page leaves out long stretches of sitting still, but it still shows any riding or driving the box recorded. To keep home out of it, switch the box on at the track. Trimming rides to just the track is on the way.

Deleting

You can delete any ride from your rider's page. To delete everything about your rider, email hello@mototoolsmx.com and we'll do it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell founding families directly.
